Street Fighter Esque Fighting Game with martial arts...

Hand Strike, Leg Strike, Grapple, Jump, left, right, duck, guard.. these are the controls.. double tap right or left for a dodge

holding punch/kick leads to a heavy variant of leg/punch.. long wind up but yea

Timed Guard leads to a parry which damages the enemy

punch/kick followed by a duck moment leads to feint

heavily based around like a dragon: black panther new chapter aka Kurohyou

each player will also have stamina.... stamina is lost when you get parried or when your attack gets dodged.. a combo will drain it but its a normal amount

p - Punch, k - Kick action, g - Grapple Action, gu - Guard Action

Characters:
Boxer = p: jab,
	k: No kick so replaced with a hook,
 	g: No Grapple replaced with uppercut, 

	combo:
 	1. p + p + k + g.. last p will be a devastating uppercut ( easy to hit );
	2. p + k + k + k.. last k will be a Dempsey roll ( the window for execution will be low, to counter the high damage )	

	Heavy Attacks:
	p: liver shot ( can stun )
	k: overhand punch ( breaks guard ) ( does big damage if not blocked )
	
	Special Actions:
	Quick Step attacks, basically a dodge can be followed by a punch
	
	forward dash + punch = dashing forward jab
	backward dash + punch = superman punch
